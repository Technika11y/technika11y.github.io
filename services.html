what we do

Accessibility compliance

For municipalities, government contractors, universities, and vendors selling into the public sector — where ADA, Section 508, and the European Accessibility Act make this a legal requirement, not a nicety.

  • PDF and web audits against WCAG 2.2 AA and PDF/UA (ISO 14289)
  • Remediation — we fix the documents and templates, not just flag them
  • A VPAT / ACR with a defensible, dated conformance claim

Food-service security & compliance

The underserved corner: connected kitchens running temperature probes, KDS screens, and a POS on one flat network, with HACCP obligations and no tooling.

  • Kitchen-IoT network assessment — zero-trust segmentation so a compromised sensor can't reach the register
  • Tamper-evident HACCP temperature evidence
  • Allergen-labelling and menu-accessibility review

how an engagement runs

  1. Scope. A short call to define the estate — which documents, which sites, which systems — and the deadline you're working to.
  2. Audit. We assess it, running the same open tools we build in the open, plus manual expert review where automation can't reach.
  3. Deliver. Remediated files and a signed VPAT/ACR, or a segmentation + HACCP report — with the findings traced to the standard they map to.
